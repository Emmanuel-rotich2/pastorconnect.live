FGCK JOYLAND APPOINTMENT TIME RULES

- Appointment duration: exactly 30 minutes.
- Fixed closing time: 3:00 PM.
- The Pastor controls the opening time.
- Whenever the opening time changes, slots are regenerated automatically from the new opening time to 3:00 PM.
- Example: opening at 11:00 AM creates exactly these 8 slots:
  11:00-11:30
  11:30-12:00
  12:00-12:30
  12:30-1:00
  1:00-1:30
  1:30-2:00
  2:00-2:30
  2:30-3:00
- Slots are generated when the member/staff schedule is opened and the availability API also detects settings changes.
- Old unbooked slots outside the new window are removed automatically. Existing booked appointments are preserved for record integrity.
