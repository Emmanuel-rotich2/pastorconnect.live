FGCK JOYLAND - PASTOR HOMEPAGE CONTENT + MEMBER DOWNLOAD

Pastor-only homepage controls:
- Mission
- Vision
- Motto
- Church Theme of the Year
- Daily Quote text
- Daily Quote date
- Daily Quote image upload (JPG, PNG, WEBP, max 5 MB)
- Publish/update/remove daily quote image

Public homepage:
- Reads the latest saved Pastor content directly from the existing settings table.
- Displays mission, vision, motto, annual theme and daily quote.
- Displays the uploaded quote image when one is published.

Pastor dashboard:
- Added Download Members action.
- Exports active members as CSV with exactly: Member Name, Phone Number, Email Address.
- Export endpoint is Pastor-authenticated and logs the download in activity_logs.

Database:
- No new database is created.
- New settings keys are added to the existing settings table by database/migration_pastor_homepage_content.sql.
