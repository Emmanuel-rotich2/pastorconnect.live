FGCK JOYLAND ROLE ROUTING FIX
==============================

The unified login now honors the role selected on the homepage role card.

Pastor card: /auth/login?role=pastor -> only active users with role=pastor are accepted -> /staff/dashboard
Church Leader card: /auth/login?role=church_leader -> /leader/dashboard
Administrator card: /auth/login?role=admin -> /admin/dashboard
Member card: /auth/login?role=member -> member account -> /member/dashboard

If /auth/login is opened without a role parameter, the database role of the
credentials determines the destination.

No new database is required for this routing fix.
