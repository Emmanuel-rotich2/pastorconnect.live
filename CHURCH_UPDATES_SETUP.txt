FGCK JOYLAND — CHURCH PICTURES & PASTOR NOTES
================================================

Added:
- Pastor Portal > Homepage Content now includes "Church Pictures & Pastor's Notes".
- Pastor can upload JPG, PNG or WEBP images up to 8 MB.
- Pastor can add an optional title and a note up to 5,000 characters.
- Published updates appear automatically on the public homepage.
- Pastor can remove previously published updates.
- Homepage shows the latest 6 published church updates.
- Uploads are stored in /uploads/church_updates/ and PHP/script execution is blocked there.

DATABASE:
Run database/migration_pastor_homepage_content.sql in the existing FGCK Joyland database.
The migration creates the homepage_church_updates table.

No existing homepage mission, vision, motto, theme, or daily quote content is removed.
