FGCK MAKUTANO WEST JOYLAND – NOTIFICATION & EMAIL WORKFLOW

1. MEMBER BOOKS AN APPOINTMENT
- The appointment is saved in MySQL.
- Every active pastor receives an in-system notification in Pastor Portal > Notifications.
- Every active pastor with a valid email receives a professional email containing member name, phone, email, date, time, purpose and appointment reference.

2. PASTOR UPDATES AN APPOINTMENT
- The member receives an in-system notification.
- The member receives an email for confirmation, decline, cancellation, completion or no-show.
- Appointment time changes can be emailed using the existing time-change helper.

3. PASTOR PUBLISHES A MESSAGE
- Published pastoral announcements, prayer messages, reminders and urgent notices are inserted into member notifications.
- Members can read them in Member Portal > Church Messages.
- Members with valid email addresses also receive the same communication by email with a reminder to log in.
- Draft messages are never emailed.

4. PASTOR PUBLISHES A CHURCH EVENT
- Members receive an in-system notification.
- The event appears in Member Portal > Events.
- All active members with valid email addresses receive the event details by email.
- Event cancellation creates an in-system notification and sends an email cancellation notice.

5. IMPORTANT EMAIL SETUP
- Configure config/email.php with valid SMTP details.
- Gmail requires a Google App Password, not the normal Gmail password.
- EMAIL_BASE_URL should be changed from localhost when the system is deployed online.

6. SECURITY
- Do not commit config/email.php to a public Git repository.
- If an App Password has ever been exposed, revoke it in Google and create a replacement.
